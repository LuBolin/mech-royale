Mech Royale · Second simulator calibration

39 passing assert-based scenarios · 57,115 schema-validated observations · 285,801 FFA ticks · 50 FFA and 50 complete practice worlds · 200 additional starter practice seeds.

Scenarios compare behavior to the written rules. Only the first-pass rows and the published starter score have independent game measurements. Each labeled regression refers to a line in sim/test_calibration.py. The in-process engine cannot exercise Docker or spectator-service rules. Counts below use the final patch and seeds 0–49, default genomes, and the supplied kit map. For full practice telemetry, the harness captured the player's normal score first, then followed survivors through the end of the same world.

The starter scored 0.305 ± 0.043 SE in the simulator versus the published 1.15. It placed 16th 152 times in 200 seeds. This limits the use of absolute practice scores. A single published mean cannot identify the per-difficulty aim, tactics or reinforcement timings. In the default-genome cohort, the zone caused 3 of 50 player deaths but 81.8% of full practice-world deaths, mostly after the player had already been scored.
